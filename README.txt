OFADA HEAVEN WEBSITE

OPEN:
Double-click index.html.

EASY EDITING:
1. Images are in /images.
2. Menu items and prices are in /js/script.js in the MENU array.
3. Main business text is directly in the HTML files.
4. Styling is in /css/style.css.
5. The cart is stored in this browser and checkout sends the whole basket in one WhatsApp message.
6. Update guest reviews in index.html as customer feedback is collected.
7. Confirm social profile URLs in index.html, menu.html, about.html and contact.html before launch.
8. Menu naira prices are in /js/script.js.
9. Reservation email is set on the reservation form in /index.html; email requests open a prefilled mail draft.
10. Google Maps embeds are in the contact sections of /index.html and /contact.html.
