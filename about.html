OUR STORY

Food with character.

Ofada Heaven brings native Nigerian flavours, rooftop evenings and Lagos energy together around one welcoming table in Surulere. We want every visit to feel relaxed, generous and worth sharing.

ROOTED IN FLAVOUR

Familiar food, full of life.

Our menu celebrates the comfort and character of Nigerian cooking, from Ofada rice and ayamase to smoky jollof and dishes made for a good evening with your people. The aim is simple: bold flavour, a warm welcome and room to enjoy the moment.

OUR KITCHEN PHILOSOPHY

Familiar flavours, prepared with care.

We believe good food should feel generous, rooted and made to be shared. Our menu celebrates the comfort and character of Nigerian cooking, from Ofada rice and ayamase to smoky jollof, with each plate part of a warm, unhurried evening.

MADE FOR GOOD EVENINGS

A rooftop table in Surulere.

Come together over a meal, take in the rooftop atmosphere and let the evening find its own pace. Whether it is a familiar favourite or a first visit, there is always space for good food and good company.
